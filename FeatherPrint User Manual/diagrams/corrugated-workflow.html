Filling the gap between walls

Corrugated replaces ordinary infill in a hollow, thin-walled part with a stringer structure that follows both walls. This is the path a part takes: what to check in the model, which settings to set, and what to look for before printing. Amber paths are correction loops.

Draft for the FeatherPrint manual, from the Corrugated specification REV 3.8 (18 September 2026) and the Corrugated settings file. Corrugated is a separate infill pattern from FeatherPrint: a mesh uses one or the other. Defaults are first-pass values not yet validated across a wide range of parts. Glob regions currently print no corrugation, and a Y-junction with no straight pass-through still corrugates each arm independently.
